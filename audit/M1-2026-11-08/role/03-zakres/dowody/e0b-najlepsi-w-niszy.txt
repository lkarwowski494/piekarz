New in 4.0:

New Features

- Community Recipes — You can now submit your recipes to the community gallery and, once approved, will appear in the Featured tab, credited to you. Community-shared recipes are always free for everyone.
- Describe Changes to This Recipe — For any recipe, type what you want changed, and Rise saves the result as a new recipe. (Available to paid users.)
- Recipe ratings — Featured recipes now display a "% liked" badge and you can like/dislike featured recipes that you make.
- Scale Recipe — a dedicated scaling UI allows you to scale in 4 different ways: by percentage, with a ½x–2x slider, to a total dough weight, or to a yield.
- Shake to Undo – Destructive actions in the app are now undoable.

UI Changes

- Redesigned for iOS 26 — Liquid Glass tab bar, toolbars, filter buttons, and an integrated search bar.
- Recipes split into two tabs — Your recipes and rises now appear together in My Baking. Curated and community recipes moved to a new Featured tab.
- On iPad, tabs and nav buttons now share one top row with the actions grouped and the sidebar toggle in a sensible place.

Workflow Improvements

- New recipes, whether added from scratch, added from Featured Recipes, or imported from the web, now always begin in My Recipes.
- You can now begin making a recipe directly from My Recipes, eliminating a step.

Smaller Improvements

- The Filter menu remains open through multiple selections, making it easier to use.
- Tapping a photo now opens the photo browser on *that* photo.
- Added a Remove Source button to the recipe source alert.
- Better task title editing - Return now splits or starts a new task and deleting an empty title removes it.
- Contacting support now works without Apple Mail configured.

Bugs fixed

- Fixed crashes: tapping an empty photo slot on iPad/Mac, and overlapping table refreshes in the rise detail view.
- Fixed onboarding hanging forever on a first launch with no network.
- Tapping a locked recipe in search results now shows the paywall instead of doing nothing.
- Fixed a runaway timer once a bake's schedule ran out.
- Fixed purchases not unlocking in some cases, and a "no trial available" error.
- Light/dark appearance now stays consistent after changing the setting.
- Fixed the missing icon on the Scale Recipe button.
- The recipes list shows proper loading and error states instead of appearing empty. 4.0.1 Aug 30 New Features

- Community Recipes — You can now submit your recipes to the community gallery and, once approved, will appear in the Featured tab, credited to you. Community-shared recipes are always free for everyone.
