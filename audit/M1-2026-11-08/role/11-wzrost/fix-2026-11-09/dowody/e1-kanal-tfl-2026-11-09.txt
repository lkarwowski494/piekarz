Breadcrumb Home Forums General Introductions and Ideas Miette – a small Android app I made to plan my bakes (feedback welcome) sbeuh on 13 Oct 2026 at 09:27 Hi everyone, I'm a developper and a home baker from France. Likemany, I was juggling recipes on scraps of paper, baker's math in a spreadsheet and several timers in my kitchen. Two years ago, I decided to put all of that in one place, and it became a small Android app called Miette (French for "crumb"). What it does, in short: - scales a formula from the final dough weight you want, and lets you adjust hydration, salt or levain in a tap - tells you before you start when the bread will be ready, then runs a timer for each step (autolyse, folds, bulk, proof, bake) - reminds you to feed your starter - includes a small set of recipes adapted for home ovens (baguettes, sourdough loaves, focaccia, rye...), some from French professional bakers, a few of my own - has a glossary of the technical terms, and lets you create and share your own recipes It's free and doesn't collect any data. Android only for now, sorry to iPhone users. It's not meant to replace anyone's judgment: an app can't tell you when the dough is ready, your hands and eyes do that. It just takes the arithmetic and the clock-watching off your mind. I'd really value feedback from people who bake more than I do: what's missing, what's confusing, what you'd never use. Anything helps. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lakademiette Thanks for reading, Sébastien Log in or register to post comments I won't let my self be insulted.
