## In short
The Baker app does not collect any data by itself. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The app does not connect to the internet (purchases are handled by the App Store) and has no account, ads, analytics or tracking. The exception is an email you choose to send us (section "When you write to us"). The controller of those messages is Łukasz Karwowski (support@apps4brew.com); your rights are listed in the same section.

## What is stored and where
Recipes, bakes and their ratings, notes, sourdough starters and their feedings, flours and settings are stored only on your device (the app's SQLite database). Nobody but you — including the app's developer — can access them. The app sends nothing.
An iPhone backup (in iCloud or on a computer), if you turn it on in iOS, also includes the app's data; it is subject to Apple's terms.

## Camera and other permissions
Version 1.0 does not use the camera, microphone, location or photos. The only permission the app may ask for is notifications.

## Purchases (subscription or lifetime purchase)
Purchases and subscriptions are handled only by Apple (App Store) — the app has no account, server or payment services and never sees payment details. On your phone it keeps only the access status read from the App Store so it can work offline; this status is never sent anywhere.

## Notifications
Reminders (end of a baking step, starter feeding) are local notifications created on the device, and only if you allow them. The app does not use push notifications and sends no data to any server.

## Backup and export
Backups (after a bake, and before importing or erasing data) and export files are saved on the device, in the app's folder (visible in the Files app). Only you share them — for example through the iOS share sheet. Once shared, a file is subject to the rules of the service you use.

## Deleting your data
You can delete your data in the app's settings ("Delete All Data"). Before deleting, the app saves a full safety backup on your phone; that backup, earlier backups, settings (language, units) and the purchase status remain — you can delete the backups in the Files app (On My iPhone → Baker). Deleting the app from your phone removes everything at once. Data already included in an iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) is removed in iOS backup settings. Exporting and deleting your own data always work, even without a purchase.

## Crash reports
If you agree in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass crash reports for the app to us under its own terms (Apple, as an independent controller, protects them under its own privacy policy). The app has no crash-reporting mechanism of its own; reports are used only to fix bugs.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## When you write to us
The app still sends nothing — "Email Us" opens your mail app with a ready message that you see and send yourself. The message includes the app version and iOS version; you can remove them before sending. If you write to support@apps4brew.com, we handle your message as follows:
- **Controller:** Łukasz Karwowski, private individual; contact: support@apps4brew.com.
- **Data:** your email address, the message, attachments, and the app and iOS versions if you leave them in.
- **Purpose:** handling your request (reply, explanation, bug fix) and organising feedback about the app.
- **Legal basis:** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest: handling requests and developing the app).
- **Retention:** 12 months after the case is closed, then we delete the message; feedback without personal data may be kept in anonymised form.
- **Recipients and processors:** Google (Google Workspace mail; Drive). Google processes it under a data processing agreement (Google Workspace Data Processing Amendment) that provides protection equal to or greater than this policy. We do not sell data, use it for advertising or pass it to artificial-intelligence tools.
- **Transfers outside the EEA:** Google may process data in the USA. The safeguards are the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the Commission's adequacy decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework) for certified companies. We will send information about the safeguards on request.
- **Your rights:** access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and a complaint to the Polish data protection authority (President of UODO). Write to support@apps4brew.com — we will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest.
- **Do you have to provide data:** writing to us is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot reply.
- **Automated decisions:** we do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

## Changes and contact
If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated before such a version is released. Questions: support@apps4brew.com.
