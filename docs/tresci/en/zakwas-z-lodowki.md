# Waking a starter from the fridge before baking (F-61, BR-ZAKW-006, RD-24) — EN
<!-- M1-audytor-generalny-104 b. EN values for the PL texts (src/lib/i18n-en/), same headings as the PL file. Role 08 implements the text verbatim (without the [T:...] marker). -->

## plan.start (zakwas w lodówce)
According to King Arthur Baking, before baking, feed the refrigerated starter in equal parts by weight and leave it at room temperature. [T:ZAK-LOD-001]
It may start bubbling quickly or take up to 12 hours, depending on when it was last fed. [T:ZAK-LOD-001]

## plan.kolejne-dokarmienia
Feed it every 12 hours until it doubles or triples in volume within 6–8 hours of feeding; then it is ready for baking. [T:ZAK-LOD-002]
The less often the starter has been fed, the longer it takes to get it ready for baking. [T:ZAK-LOD-003]

## niegotowy-w-dniu-ciasta (BR-ZAKW-006)
The starter has not doubled yet: according to King Arthur Baking, feed it again and check after the next feeding whether it doubles within 6–8 hours. [T:ZAK-LOD-002]
You can move the bake plan. [T:-]

## calculateStarterPhase.advicePl, faza lag (lodówka, przed progiem)
According to King Arthur Baking, before baking, feed the starter from the fridge and leave it at room temperature; it may take up to 12 hours to show signs of life. [T:ZAK-LOD-001]
