## brioche plan, step brioche_mix, category mix (namePl)
Kneading the dough with butter [T:-]

## brioche plan, step brioche_mix (instructionsPl; followed by the two sentences BEZ-SUR-001, BEZ-SUR-002 as in the mix step)
Combine the flour, water, salt and starter and knead. [T:-]
Add the butter gradually, a little at a time. [T:PIE-BRI-002]
When all the butter has blended into the dough, keep kneading until the dough starts to pull away from the sides of the bowl. [T:PIE-BRI-002]

## brioche plan, step brioche_cold, category bulk_ferment (namePl)
Dough overnight in the fridge [T:-]

## brioche plan, step brioche_cold (instructionsPl)
Put the dough on a baking sheet lined with buttered baking paper. [T:PIE-BRI-002]
Cover it tightly with plastic wrap and put it in the fridge overnight. [T:PIE-BRI-002]

## brioche plan, step brioche_shape, category final_shape (namePl)
Shaping in the loaf pan [T:-]

## brioche plan, step brioche_shape (instructionsPl)
Roll the dough by hand to the length of the loaf pan. [T:PIE-BRI-002]
Lightly butter the loaf pan and put the dough in it. [T:PIE-BRI-002]

## brioche plan, step brioche_proof, category final_proof (namePl)
Rising in the loaf pan [T:-]

## brioche plan, step brioche_proof (instructionsPl)
Cover the loaf pan with plastic wrap and leave the dough to rise in a warm place. [T:PIE-BRI-002]
