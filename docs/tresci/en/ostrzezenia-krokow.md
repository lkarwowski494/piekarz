## starter: starter_feed, rye_starter, feeding and creating a starter (RD-22-W2)
Do not taste raw starter or flour. [T:ZAK-SUR-001,BEZ-SUR-001]
After handling flour and starter, wash your hands with soap under running water. [T:BEZ-SUR-002]

## hot pot: preheat_oven, bake, lid_on, rye_bake, cool, rye_cool (RD-23-W2, RD-23-W4)
Handle the hot pot, lid and tin with dry oven mitts or pot holders. [T:BEZ-OPA-004,BEZ-OPA-003]

## children: preheat_oven, bake, lid_on, rye_bake (RD-23-W2)
Keep children away from the stove and the area where you are working. [T:BEZ-OPA-005]

## unknown kind: a step without a kind from the user's own recipe (RD-22-W4)
If this step involves raw flour, dough or starter, do not taste them, and wash your hands with soap under running water afterwards. [T:BEZ-SUR-001,ZAK-SUR-001,BEZ-SUR-002]
