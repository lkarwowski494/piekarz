## executionStepsEn[0]
According to King Arthur Baking, dust the basket with a light but thorough layer of flour: any flour will do, from all-purpose and bread flour to whole wheat. [T:KOS-OPR-001]

## executionStepsEn[1]
According to the same source, many bakers choose a coarser flour, such as semolina or rice flour, because it forms a clearer barrier between the dough and the basket and does not work into the dough. [T:KOS-OPR-002]
