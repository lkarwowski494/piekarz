## diagnostics.GUMMY_CRUMB.biologicalCausePl
Bread taken out of the oven is not done yet: according to King Arthur Baking, it releases moisture as it cools and the crumb needs that time to set, so if sliced too early it may be gummy. [T:KRO-POC-015]
Another cause may be underbaking: according to King Arthur Baking, most loaves are done when the centre reaches about 88 °C. [T:DIA-TEM-001]

## diagnostics.GUMMY_CRUMB.actionChecklistPl[0]
Before slicing, wait until the loaf has cooled completely. [T:KRO-POC-015]
According to King Arthur Baking, a baked sourdough rye bread should rest for 24 hours before slicing, for best quality. [T:ZYT-STU-001]

## diagnostics.GUMMY_CRUMB.actionChecklistPl[1]
Check the temperature in the centre of the loaf with a probe thermometer: according to King Arthur Baking, most loaves are done at about 88 °C. [T:DIA-TEM-001]

## diagnostics.GUMMY_CRUMB.actionChecklistPl[2]
If the centre is cooler, bake longer and measure again. [T:DIA-TEM-001]

## diagnostics.UNDER_PROOFED.actionChecklistPl[2]
Work out the water temperature in the recipe: according to King Arthur Baking, wheat dough should be about 24–25.5 °C after mixing. [T:DIA-DDT-001]

## diagnostics.OVER_PROOFED.actionChecklistPl[2]
Lower the fridge temperature: according to King Arthur Baking, a lower temperature slows fermentation. [T:KRO-POC-010]

## diagnostics.SIDE_BLOWOUT.actionChecklistPl[0]
Bake covered in a pot: according to King Arthur Baking, steam under the lid keeps the crust soft longer, so the loaf can keep rising at the start of baking. [T:KRO-POC-013]

## diagnostics.SIDE_BLOWOUT.actionChecklistPl[1]
Hold a curved blade at about 30° to the dough surface and cut about 6–13 mm deep: according to King Arthur Baking, a shallower cut may fuse back together during baking. [T:DIA-NAC-001]

## diagnostics.LACK_OF_EAR.biologicalCausePl
According to King Arthur Baking, a straight-on cut at 90° splays open to the sides, while an ear forms with a cut at a shallow angle, from the taut outer layer of dough shaped with sufficient tension. [T:DIA-NAC-002]

## diagnostics.LACK_OF_EAR.actionChecklistPl[0]
Tilt a curved blade to about 30° to the dough surface: according to King Arthur Baking, this promotes a flap of dough that peels back into an ear. [T:DIA-NAC-001,DIA-NAC-002]

## diagnostics.LACK_OF_EAR.actionChecklistPl[2]
Preheat the empty pot before putting the dough in: according to King Arthur Baking, about 30 minutes, so that steam forms in it. [T:KRO-POC-011]

## diagnostics.PALE_CRUST.actionChecklistPl[0]
After removing the lid, keep baking: according to King Arthur Baking, the uncovered bread bakes until it is fully browned. [T:KRO-POC-014]

## diagnostics.EXCESSIVELY_SOUR.actionChecklistPl[0]
Feed the starter more often before baking. [T:KWA-GLO-001]

## diagnostics.EXCESSIVELY_SOUR.actionChecklistPl[2]
Shorten the bulk fermentation and watch the dough temperature: according to King Arthur Baking, wheat dough should be about 24–25.5 °C after mixing. [T:KWA-CZA-001,DIA-DDT-001]

## diagnostics.BLAND_FLAVOR.actionChecklistPl[0]
Proof the dough in the fridge: according to King Arthur Baking, cold slows fermentation, and slower fermentation deepens the flavour. [T:KRO-POC-010]

## diagnostics.DENSE_CRUMB.actionChecklistPl[2]
Extend the autolyse and add an extra set of coil folds. [T:KRO-POC-003,KRO-POC-006]
