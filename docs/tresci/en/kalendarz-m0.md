## piekarnik.zajety.nagrzewanie (arkusz kolizji, BR-KAL-016)
Preheating ties up the oven too: an empty pot takes about 30 minutes, a baking stone usually about 60. [T:KRO-POC-011,PIE-ZAJ-001]

## piekarnik.zajety.grupowanie (propozycja przesunięcia, BR-KAL-016)
It's worth baking several things on the same day, one after another, since an oven with a stone takes a long time to heat up. [T:PIE-ZAJ-002]

## karmienie.staly-dzien.opis (BR-KAL-010)
A starter kept in the refrigerator is fed once a week. [T:ZAK-ZAL-012]
After feeding, leave it at room temperature for 1–2 hours, closer to 2 in a cool kitchen, then put it back in the fridge. [T:ZAK-LOD-005]

## karmienie.staly-dzien.warunek (przed włączeniem stałego dnia)
A starter goes into the refrigerator only 7–10 days after it was created. [T:ZAK-ZAL-012]

## karmienie.staly-dzien.przesuniecie (gdy termin wypada niewygodnie)
Feeding doesn't have to happen on the dot. [T:ZAK-LOD-006]

## karmienie.staly-dzien.pominiete (minął dzień karmienia)
A starter that's missed a day, two or even a week of feeding can almost certainly be revived by feeding it every 12 hours and keeping it at room temperature until it's healthy again. [T:ZAK-LOD-007]
The less often a starter has been fed, the longer it takes to get it ready for baking. [T:ZAK-LOD-003]
