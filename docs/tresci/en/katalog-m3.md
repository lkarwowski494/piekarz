## durum_semolina_sourdough.name
Durum semolina sourdough bread [T:-]

## durum_semolina_sourdough.description
Bread made from durum wheat semolina, water, sea salt and sourdough starter, without baker's yeast. [T:KAT-ALT-007]
The formula comes from the specification of the protected designation of origin "Pane di Altamura". [T:KAT-ALT-001]

## durum_semolina_sourdough.notes
Amounts and temperatures are given as in the source's production description, not converted for a home kitchen or home oven. [T:-]
According to the specification, 100 kg of semolina takes 20 kg of sourdough starter, 2 kg of sea salt and about 60 l of water at 18 °C. [T:KAT-ALT-001]
In baker's percentages, that is 60% water, 2% salt and 20% starter by semolina weight. [T:KAT-ALT-007]
Before use, the starter is refreshed at least three times with water and durum semolina. [T:KAT-ALT-002]
According to the specification, mixing takes 20 minutes in a mixer with plunging arms. [T:KAT-ALT-003]
Covered with a cloth, the dough ferments for at least 90 minutes, rests 30 minutes after the first shaping and at least 15 minutes after the second. [T:KAT-ALT-004]
According to the specification, the oven reaches 250 °C, and the bread bakes for the first 15 minutes with the oven open and another 45 minutes with it closed. [T:KAT-ALT-005]

## latvian_scalded_rye.name
Rye bread with scalded flour on a starter [T:-]

## latvian_scalded_rye.description
A rye bread made with a starter, in which part of the flour is scalded with hot water and caraway and sweetened with rye malt, without baker's yeast. [T:KAT-RUP-001,KAT-RUP-002,KAT-RUP-006]
The formula comes from the specification of the traditional speciality guaranteed "Salinātā rudzu rupjmaize". [T:KAT-RUP-006]

## latvian_scalded_rye.notes
Amounts and temperatures are given as in the source's production description, not converted for a home kitchen or home oven. [T:-]
According to the specification, in a recipe for 10 kg of flour, 3 kg of coarse rye flour is scalded with 0.08–0.1 kg of caraway and 6–8 l of hot water at 85–95 °C. [T:KAT-RUP-001]
Water this hot scalds: even water at 150 °F, about 66 °C, gives most adults third-degree burns in 2 seconds. [T:BEZ-WRZ-001]
After scalding, the mixture is 63–68 °C. [T:KAT-RUP-001]
The scalded flour with 50–300 g of unfermented rye malt is held at 63–65 °C for 2–4 hours, then cooled and fermented for 12–24 hours. [T:KAT-RUP-002]
To 7–10 kg of the cooled scalded flour, 0.4–1.6 kg of starter is added, and it ferments for 3–6 hours at 35–36 °C. [T:KAT-RUP-003]
The dough is made of 7–12 kg of fermented scalded flour, 7–10 kg of rye flour, 0.8–1.6 kg of sugar and 0.15–0.2 kg of salt, and it ferments for 2–3 hours at 30–34 °C. [T:KAT-RUP-004]
At most 10% wheat flour may be added. [T:KAT-RUP-004]
In baker's percentages, with 10 kg of flour as 100%, water is 60–80%, salt 1.5–2%, sugar 8–16%, caraway 0.8–1%, malt 0.5–3% and starter 4–16%. [T:KAT-RUP-007]
The loaves are baked on the hearth first at 280–350 °C, then at 200–250 °C, for about 1–2 hours in total depending on size. [T:KAT-RUP-005]
After baking, they are brushed with starch paste or water. [T:KAT-RUP-005]
