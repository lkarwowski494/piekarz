## machine.capacity
Enter the largest amount of flour from your machine's manual, and Piekarz will not scale the recipe above it. [T:MAS-062]
For example, the Tefal PF220838 takes at most 620 g of bread flour and 10 g of dry yeast. [T:MAS-002]

## machine.order
Your machine's manual gives the order for adding ingredients. [T:MAS-060]
Most manuals we read put liquids in first, then flour and other dry ingredients, and the yeast on top so it does not touch the liquids. [T:MAS-005,MAS-020,MAS-040,MAS-050]
Some machines work the other way round: dry yeast first, flour and dry ingredients on it, and the liquid last. [T:MAS-031]
In a machine with a yeast dispenser, dry yeast goes into the dispenser. [T:MAS-030]

## machine.delayBlocked
Delayed start is off because the recipe has eggs, milk, cheese, yogurt or fresh fruit. [T:MAS-072]
Machine manuals forbid delayed start with these ingredients because they spoil easily. [T:MAS-072,MAS-009,MAS-035]
Dry milk instead of fresh milk does not turn off delayed start. [T:MAS-084]

## machine.safety
After baking, take the pan out with oven mitts, because the pan, its handle and the inside of the lid are hot. [T:MAS-010,MAS-024,MAS-036,MAS-053]
A paddle left in the loaf may be very hot: remove it after the bread cools, with the supplied tool or a plastic spatula, not a knife or fork. [T:MAS-045,MAS-025,MAS-037,MAS-013]
Hot steam may escape when you open the lid during or at the end of a program. [T:MAS-011,MAS-026,MAS-038]
Keep children under 8 away from the machine and its cord, and let older children use it only under supervision. [T:MAS-014,MAS-038]

## machine.programHint
The program hint is a Piekarz rule, not the maker's advice, so check it against your machine's manual. [T:-]
We suggest the dough program for breads shaped and baked in the oven, because in it the machine only kneads and does not bake. [T:MAS-006,MAS-046]
We suggest the sweet program for sweet recipes, because it is made for breads with a lot of sugar, fat and protein. [T:MAS-083]
We suggest the whole wheat program when wholegrain and rye flour together make up at least 50% of the flour. [T:MAS-042]
We suggest the French program for recipes without sugar or fat. [T:MAS-082]
For other recipes we suggest the basic program, meant mainly for white wheat flour. [T:MAS-081]

## machine.sourdough
We found no manual or source describing baking bread with sourdough alone in a bread machine. [T:-]
So in machine mode this recipe uses its yeast version. [T:-]
