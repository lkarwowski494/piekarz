## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.titlePl
Over-proofed dough [T:-]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.triggerSummaryPl
If the dough is over-proofed, a gentle fingertip dent stays put. [T:RAT-PRZ-001]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.immediateActionPl
Reshape yeast dough: a badly over-proofed yeast loaf baked straight away collapses in the oven, though it still tastes good. [T:RAT-PRZ-002]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.stepByStepPl[0]
Sourdough usually can't be saved this way: it rarely works, and it's only worth a try if the dough was made and proofed within a few hours. [T:RAT-PRZ-004,RAT-PRZ-005]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.stepByStepPl[1]
Sourdough proofed overnight in the cold turns tough once degassed: the loaf will rise poorly, come out misshapen and taste very sour. [T:RAT-PRZ-004]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.stepByStepPl[2]
If you're saving the dough, take it out of the basket or bowl. [T:RAT-PRZ-003]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.stepByStepPl[3]
Degas it by pressing down firmly, as you do when shaping. [T:RAT-PRZ-003]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.stepByStepPl[4]
Shape it again and let it proof. [T:RAT-PRZ-003]

## OVERPROOFED_COLLAPSE.scientificReasoningPl
Over-proofed dough collapses because the gluten has weakened and there's too much gas. [T:RAT-PRZ-006]

## SLACK_HIGH_HYDRATION.titlePl
Dough too slack [T:-]

## SLACK_HIGH_HYDRATION.triggerSummaryPl
The dough is sticky and loose and won't hold its shape. [T:-]

## SLACK_HIGH_HYDRATION.immediateActionPl
Check the salt went in: dough without salt is sticky and slack, because salt holds moisture and tightens the gluten. [T:RAT-SOL-001]

## SLACK_HIGH_HYDRATION.stepByStepPl[0]
If the salt is missing, open the "Forgotten salt" protocol. [T:-]

## SLACK_HIGH_HYDRATION.stepByStepPl[1]
Stretch and fold the dough during fermentation: it helps build the gluten network and strengthens the dough. [T:KRO-POC-006]

## SLACK_HIGH_HYDRATION.stepByStepPl[2]
With 100% rye bread you can usually skip the folds. [T:KRO-POC-006]

## SLACK_HIGH_HYDRATION.scientificReasoningPl
Folding during fermentation helps build the gluten network and strengthens the dough, and salt tightens the gluten. [T:KRO-POC-006,RAT-SOL-001]

## COLD_SLUGGISH.titlePl
Sluggish fermentation (Cold kitchen) [T:-]

## COLD_SLUGGISH.triggerSummaryPl
The dough is rising more slowly than usual: often it's just sitting somewhere cool. [T:RAT-ZIM-001]

## COLD_SLUGGISH.immediateActionPl
Move the dough somewhere warmer: yeast does best at about 24–25.5 °C. [T:RAT-ZIM-002]

## COLD_SLUGGISH.stepByStepPl[2]
Other warm spots: the top of the fridge or a high shelf, since heat rises. [T:RAT-ZIM-003]

## COLD_SLUGGISH.stepByStepPl[3]
Cover the bowl with a lid or cling film: the moisture keeps the surface of the dough soft and helps it rise. [T:RAT-ZIM-004]

## COLD_SLUGGISH.stepByStepPl[4]
Go by how the dough looks, not the clock: it rises more slowly in cold air, and recipe times are only a guide. [T:RAT-ZIM-005]

## COLD_SLUGGISH.scientificReasoningPl
In the 20–26 °C range a simple rule works: 6 °C cooler (say 20 instead of 26 °C) means fermentation takes about 2 times as long. [T:FER-Q10-002]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.titlePl
Forgotten salt [T:-]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.triggerSummaryPl
The dough is already mixed, and the weighed salt is still on the counter. [T:-]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.immediateActionPl
Add the salt before the first rise: yeast dough without salt rises faster than usual, tastes flatter and has a weaker structure. [T:RAT-SOL-002,RAT-SOL-005]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.stepByStepPl[0]
Don't taste the dough to check the salt: raw dough shouldn't be eaten or tasted, because raw flour can carry harmful bacteria. [T:BEZ-SUR-001]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.stepByStepPl[1]
Mix the salt with a few teaspoons of water. [T:RAT-SOL-003]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.stepByStepPl[2]
Press the dough out flat, spread the mixture over it, roll it up, press it out again, rotate it 90° and roll it up again. [T:RAT-SOL-003]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.stepByStepPl[3]
Knead until the dough is fairly smooth again. [T:RAT-SOL-003]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.stepByStepPl[4]
With sourdough you can treat the pause as an autolyse: after a 20–60 minute rest, mix in the salt and carry on with the recipe. [T:RAT-SOL-004]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.stepByStepPl[5]
If you only notice the missing salt after the first rise, adding it won't help and kneading again can ruin the dough for good: bake it without salt or use it for a pizza base. [T:RAT-SOL-005]

## FORGOTTEN_SALT.scientificReasoningPl
Salt slows fermentation, holds moisture and tightens the gluten. [T:RAT-SOL-001,RAT-SOL-002]
