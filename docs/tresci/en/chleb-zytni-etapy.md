## wariant.opis
The rye variant has no dough folding stage. [T:ZYT-ETA-001]
After shaping, the dough rises in a tin and is baked in it. [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## wariant.prog (variant selection condition)
The rye variant applies to dough in which rye flour makes up more than 50% of the flour. [T:ZYT-GRA-001]

## rye_starter.name
Rye sourdough [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## rye_mix.name
Mixing the dough [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## rye_mix.instructions
Mix the ingredients into a uniform mass. [T:ZYT-ETA-003]

## rye_rest.name
Short dough fermentation [T:ZYT-ETA-004]

## rye_shape.name
Dividing and shaping [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## rye_shape.instructions
Divide the dough, shape it by hand and place it in a tin. [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## rye_proof.name
Rise in the tin [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## rye_proof.instructions
Leave the dough in the tin until its surface reaches the rim of the tin. [T:ZYT-ETA-005]

## rye_bake.name
Baking in the tin [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## rye_bake.instructions
Provide steam in the oven at the start of baking. [T:ZYT-ETA-006]

## rye_cool.name
Cooling [T:ZYT-ETA-001]

## rye_cool.instructions
According to King Arthur Baking, a baked sourdough rye bread should rest for 24 hours before slicing, for best quality. [T:ZYT-STU-001]
