## plan, step autolyse (instructionsPl)
Combine the flour and water, cover and set aside: the flour absorbs the water and gluten starts to form. [T:KRO-POC-003]

## plan, step bulk_ferment (instructionsPl)
Stretch and fold the dough during fermentation. [T:KRO-POC-006]

## plan, step final_proof (instructionsPl)
Put the basket in a sealed bag in the fridge. [T:RAT-ZIM-004]
The cold of the fridge slows fermentation, and slower fermentation means deeper flavor. [T:KRO-POC-010]

## plan, sleep-collision hint (autoResolve, suggestion)
Suggested fix: extend the proof in the fridge or move the starter feeding time to avoid night-time steps. [T:-]
