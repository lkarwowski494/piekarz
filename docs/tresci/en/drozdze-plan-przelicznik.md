# Yeast: fresh → dry conversion (D-172) and plan for the yeast version (D-155, D-174) — EN
<!-- Role 06, E2, 2026-11-03. Same headers and claims as docs/tresci/pl/drozdze-plan-przelicznik.md. -->

## yeast.conversionNote
The app counts dry yeast, instant and active, as 0.35 of the weight of fresh yeast. [T:DRO-INS-030]
This is the middle between two groups of makers' values, 0.28 and 0.417, and some give even less, so your yeast package may say otherwise. [T:DRO-INS-030]

## yeast.dryHandling
Instant yeast can go straight into the flour. [T:DRO-INS-005,DRO-INS-016]
Some makers say to dissolve active dry yeast in warm water first. [T:DRO-INS-011,DRO-INS-012,DRO-INS-016]

## plan.yeast.steps
A yeast bake has these steps: mixing, kneading, first rise, degassing, shaping, second rise and baking. [T:DRO-MOD-016]
The plan takes its times from the recipe. [T:-]
Times are a guide, because the rise depends on the ingredients, dough strength, temperature and humidity. [T:DRO-MOD-016]

## plan.yeast.fingerTest
The dough is ready when a finger dent stays. [T:DRO-MOD-013]
Before baking, the shaped dough should not more than double in size. [T:DRO-MOD-015]
